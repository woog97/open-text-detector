Reading it

    Recall uses each model's own threshold: 1% false positives on the main eval set's dev human sentences. "Human false-positive rate" is the share of the set's human control sentences flagged at that threshold. Small-edit sets are small (about ±3–4 points); every run is one seed. Fable polish is left out (2 AI sentences).
