Per-work license gate:
An explicit front-matter statement that this work is available/licensed/distributed under a license must occur within the first 30,000 characters.
Its nearby license URI must identify CC BY 2.0/3.0/4.0, optionally IGO.
NC, ND, noncommercial, and no-derivatives grants are excluded.
A cited Creative Commons URL alone never establishes permission for the work.
Exact grant context, source offsets and canonical license URL are retained with each original.
Required author/title/URL/DOI metadata stays attached; no blanket repository-wide license is inferred.
