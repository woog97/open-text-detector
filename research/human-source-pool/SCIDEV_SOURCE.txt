SciDev historical news candidate adapter
=======================================
Source: official English/global/news SciDev URLs from frozen
common-pile/cccc_filtered revision03a3de5713a0bb23267d26724346508af0f25327.
Only pre2022 warc_date captures are eligible. Auxiliary wrappers retain exact
original CCCC record, source file/row, upstream JSON-line and archive hashes.
The parent CCCC collector retains original shards separately. SciDev snapshots
materialize its auxiliary table under a pinned SQLite read transaction.

Current official pages serve only to corroborate article identity, attribution,
and article-specific republish terms. HTML bytes/checksums and parsed evidence
are preserved separately from historical text. The importer requires the actual
republish popup to say this article may be republished under a Creative Commons
Attribution license, a named byline in the republish article HTML, and a pre2022
publication date. The schema author 'Editor' is not treated as the writer.
Version remains unspecified because the current article notice does not state
one and publisher policy pages disagree between CC BY2.0 and3.0. No uniform
license URI is invented. Current notice/byline are explicitly labeled current,
not evidence that the 2026 HTML was captured before2022.

Text matching uses monotonic exact normalized word-token blocks of at least8
words; at least120 matched tokens and50% current body coverage are required.
Only matched historical regions supply data, with at most3 unmatched tokens
between adjacent matching blocks. Up to3 nonoverlapping contiguous sentence
spans per article are selected with unchanged character offsets and source text.
No current-only paragraphs are copied into the human dataset. Newspaper wire
bylines and explicit external republication indicators are rejected. Image,
table, script and caption material is excluded from the supporting body.

Run on existing training Space only:
ingest_scidev.py --base /tmp/pangram-scidev-staging-20261002 \
 --aux-db /tmp/pangram-cccc-staging-20261002/stage.sqlite3 --quota991 --workers4
(Use separate argument tokens: --quota 991 --workers 4.)
The stage is isolated. Preserve scidev.lock, process identity, stage.sqlite3,
source-downloads, progress, and exports. Never merge without global dedup and
source quotas. Candidate status remains quarantined; current terms and overlap
checks do not certify training admission. A shortage of1000-word captures must
remain an explicit bin deficit, not padded documents or a quota substitution.

Four meaningful tests pass locally/remotely: URL/date eligibility, article-level
notice/byline restrictions, unrelated-body and navigation rejection, exact
original offsets/nonoverlap/cap with separate evidence provenance.
