Extraction rules:
Pre-2022 records with source-provided public-domain evidence; explicitly selected English channel names plus lexical/non-Latin checks.
One passage per original channel/day document, with exact character offsets into unchanged full logs.
Only timestamped <nickname> turns are considered. System/action lines, known bot nicknames, shell/bot commands, excessive links and low-prose lines split conversations.
Long identical repeated messages are excluded as bot-like; gaps over 20 minutes split conversations.
No removed bot/system line can remain between selected human turns.
Conversation spans retain original timestamps and nicknames; they are not flattened into invented prose.
Dates, channel/day identity, speaker family hashes and original nickname attribution are retained for grouping and provenance.
Unknown bots cannot be guaranteed absent by heuristics. All rows stay quarantined, with explicit authorship, privacy, protected-overlap and extraction-review flags.
