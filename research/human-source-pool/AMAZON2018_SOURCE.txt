Original Amazon Review Data 2018 collection adapter
=================================================
Publisher: https://cseweb.ucsd.edu/~jmcauley/datasets/amazon_v2/index.html
Full-category originals: https://mcauleylab.ucsd.edu/public_datasets/data/amazon_v2/categoryFiles/
Do not substitute five-core releases or Amazon Reviews 2023. Publisher links were
verified in the official HTML saved on the training Space. The source is the
233.1M-review 2018 release (May 1996-October 2018), regardless of host object
modification dates. Per-review timestamps must remain at or before 2018-10-31.

Run: ingest_amazon2018.py --base /tmp/pangram-amazon2018-stage-v1 --quota 5306 --workers 4
Only the existing Space holds source datasets. Stage SQLite is isolated; do not
merge until stage validation succeeds and global deduplication checks pass.
Each worker makes bounded sequential reads from original gzip streams; retained
compressed prefixes are intentionally not complete gzip archives. Manifests
record that fact, bytes, checksum, URL, ETag, last-modified time, and rows read.
Each accepted review preserves the exact original JSON line, SHA256, row number,
uncompressed byte offset, full original fields, unchanged review text, source
and category tags, reviewer/product grouping, rating and purchase verification.
Validation decodes every selected line from its retained original compressed
prefix and checks the original line hash and byte offset. No summaries or
product descriptions are sampled as customer reviews.

Ten product areas: books, electronics, home/kitchen, clothing/shoes/jewelry,
beauty, sports/outdoors, toys/games, office, pets, movies/TV. Each reserves at least
250 reviews; each may supply at most 796 (15% rounded up). Each category reserves
20 reviews per star rating. No individual rating may exceed 478 per category.
All 5,306 reviewers are distinct; products supply at most three reviews. These
are within-source selection controls, not changes to the agreed source quota.
Review length quotas follow the existing reviews mix: 1,857 short, 2,388 medium,
955 long and 106 very long. Parallel network readers share a bounded in-memory
quota gate and only hold SQLite transactions for individual insertions.

Candidates remain quarantined. The approved research release does not establish
commercial training rights to underlying customer reviews. Fake/incentivized
reviews, privacy, benchmark overlap and product/reviewer grouping remain audit
flags. Prefix sampling has ordering bias; product-area and rating coverage do
not establish a random representative sample of all Amazon customers.
