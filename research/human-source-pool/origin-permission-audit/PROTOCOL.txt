Scope: Attempt human-origin and permission-to-use checks on every one of the
100,000 Stage 1 passages. English only. No other stages, filtering, deletion,
generation, training or admission decisions.

Completion: Both checks attempted and a result written for every record.
Passed, failed and could_not_verify all count as a completed attempt.

Human origin: Compare every passage and its hash to the saved original. Inspect
its source identity, original archive identity, publication attribution, capture
dates and fixed-revision evidence. Historical corpora and attributable original
publications can support a passed result. Old claimed dates on mutable pages do
not by themselves establish historical wording. No AI detector or style guess.
Passed means supporting provenance evidence, not mathematical proof that no
automation was ever involved.

Permission: Check preserved source-specific licenses against published source
terms fetched during this audit. Scope is reusable training text, including
commercial use, under applicable conditions. A noncommercial restriction on the
downloaded release fails this broad-use check; it need not prohibit research use.
Preserve conflicting grants rather than selecting the most permissive one.
Mirror-only work-license assertions without independently established original
grants, missing license versions, third-party review rights and inconsistent
policies receive could_not_verify. All such attempts are complete, not pending.
No individual original-work page was fetched for every mirrored article/book.

The result is evidence-based dataset annotation, not blanket legal clearance or
an assertion that attribution/share-alike obligations have already been fulfilled.
Evidence URLs, fetch receipts/hashes, input hashes, decision reasons and code
version are preserved. No source passage text is copied to the local computer.

Original intake flags are historical and remain unchanged. The new fields
human_origin_check_completed/result and permission_to_use_check_completed/result
are authoritative for completion/results of these two checks. A failed or
unverified result does not leave the task unfinished. All other fields and
training eligibility are outside this task and unchanged.
